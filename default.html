What We Do

Comprehensive Marine Fabrication

Engineered for strength, durability, and sleek aesthetics in harsh salt-water environments.

Specialized TIG Welding

High-precision TIG welding for aluminium and stainless steel. Strong, seamless welds engineered to withstand marine stress and vibration.

Aluminium Fabrication

Custom aluminium hardtops, hulls, platforms, and fuel tanks fabricated using marine-grade 5083 alloy for structural integrity and light weight.

Stainless Steel Railings

Custom 316 stainless bow rails, pushpits, rocket launchers, and grab handles polished to a high mirror finish for corrosion resistance.

Custom Boat Hardtops & T-Tops

Tailor-made Bimini frames, canvas support structures, and solid aluminium hardtops integrated directly with your vessel layout.

Custom Fuel Tanks

Pressure-tested custom aluminium and stainless fuel & water tanks made to exact dimensions to maximize internal hull space.

Repairs & Modification

Structural crack repairs, bow roller additions, transom modifications, and custom accessory mounts performed in-shop or on-site.
